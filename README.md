⚡ Electricity Saver


🌱 About the Project


Electricity Saver is a web-based project focused on Environmental Sustainability. It helps users monitor their electricity usage, identify potential energy wastage, estimate electricity costs, and receive practical suggestions for reducing unnecessary power consumption.
The project aims to encourage energy-efficient habits and help users make better decisions about their electricity usage.

🎯 Objectives

i) Monitor and analyze electricity consumption.
ii) Estimate electricity usage and cost.
iii) Promote sustainable and responsible energy consumption.
iv) Help users understand the impact of their electricity usage.

✨ Features

i) Electricity consumption monitoring.
ii) Usage analysis and dashboard.
iii) User-friendly web interface.

🌍 Environmental Impact

Unnecessary electricity consumption contributes to increased energy demand and can indirectly increase greenhouse-gas emissions depending on how electricity is generated.
Electricity Saver encourages users to:
 i) Reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.
 ii) Identify inefficient usage patterns.
 iii) Switch off unused appliances.
 iv) Develop energy-efficient habits.
 v) Reduce avoidable electricity expenses.
